Frequently asked

The questions every CEO
and VP of lending asks.

Isn't this just indirect lending with a digital wrapper?

No. It's the structural opposite. In indirect lending, the dealer originates the loan and you buy the paper — meaning the dealer controls the relationship and the F&I office can pitch captive financing for higher commissions. With Carscu, the member pre-approves with you first, shops in your branded marketplace, and dealers in the network are contractually unable to offer alternative financing. You own the loan, the rate, and the member relationship end to end. Same demand. Dramatically less leakage.

Why would dealers agree to a no-flip arrangement?

Because Carscu delivers them a pre-approved, pre-qualified buyer with the financing already locked. Dealers in the network exchange their F&I flip-margin for a much higher close rate, faster funding, and zero financing-related cancellations. For volume-focused dealers, that math wins. The agreement is part of network onboarding — there is no opt-out at the deal level.

How fast can a credit union go live?

Weeks, not quarters. Standard onboarding includes branded marketplace setup, loan-officer access provisioning, and integration with your existing application intake — your underwriting and core system don't change. We'll walk through specifics on the call.

Does Carscu work with our core system?

Carscu sits alongside your core — Symitar, Episys, Corelation, MeridianLink, and others — without replacing it. Your loan application, underwriting, and funding flows continue inside your existing systems. Carscu owns the marketplace experience and dealer-side controls.

Is this a fit for California credit unions specifically?

Yes. Carscu is built for California credit union lending teams as well as credit unions nationwide. California credit unions face especially high captive-financing pressure given the state's auto-buying volume and franchise dealer concentration — which makes Carscu's no-flip mechanism even more impactful here. The platform is fully white-labeled to your credit union's brand, regardless of region.
